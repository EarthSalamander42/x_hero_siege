# X Hero Siege 4.0

> Draft for human review before publication.

Version 4.0 is a large systems and encounter update. The biggest player-facing change is Phase 3: bosses now use dedicated encounter AI, readable telegraphs, cast bars, timers, counters, and difficulty-scaled pattern decks instead of mostly static scripting. The update also adds the rune system, Fragment Quests, Supporter Pass foundations, a new top HUD, a richer end screen, new notifications, item/orb updates, and a broad content pipeline cleanup.

## Highlights

- Phase 3 boss fights were rebuilt around modern encounter rules: dynamic pattern selection, visible warning zones, cast bars, HP markers, timers, and mechanics that scale by difficulty.
- New rune system: Recovery, Defense, Offense, and Misc runes can appear during special wave windows and create short team-wide or nearby power spikes.
- New Fragment Quests: optional match objectives track team healing, damage, potion discipline, event performance, boss kills, deaths, base health, orb variety, and more.
- Supporter Pass groundwork is now visible in-game with tier identity, fragments, XP progress, settings, profile elements, and end-screen support displays.
- The HUD was rebuilt with ally status, supporter marks, ankh/reincarnation visibility, event timers, special wave countdowns, overhead crowd-control labels, and cleaner boss bars.
- Items and orbs received a major pass with new active/off states, new Arcane/Venom/Wind orb families, refreshed visuals, and clearer icon coverage.
- Special events and arenas now have more explicit timers, teleport/return handling, reward flyouts, and Fragment Quest hooks.

## Phase 3 Boss Encounters

- Phase 3 bosses now share a dedicated AI framework for target selection, cooldown handling, movement, pattern weighting, warning telegraphs, and difficulty scaling.
- Boss mechanics are designed to stay recognizable across difficulties while damage, delays, density, thresholds, and pressure scale upward.
- Boss health bars now support cast bars, delayed health feedback, heal pulses, counters, timers, HP markers, multi-boss states, and special mechanics such as ankh counters.
- Grom Hellscream now uses an encounter deck built around Mirror Trial, Blade Storm, Mirror Cleave, Windwalk Ambush, Warsong Leap, and Blood Frenzy.
- Illidan now has a dedicated Phase 3 kit with Fel Beam, Shadow Dash, Immolation Burst, Glaive Storm, and Metamorphosis pressure.
- Balanar now uses Nightfall, Dread Howl, Sleeping Terror, Carrion Swarm, Rain of Chaos, and Vampiric Presence as visible encounter patterns.
- Proudmoore now has naval-themed pressure through Admiral's Command, Torrent Line, Broadside, Anchor Smash, and Focus Fire.
- Arthas now has a dedicated Phase 3 script with Frostmourne Mark, Frozen Chains, Death Advance, and Frostmourne Execute.
- The Lich King now uses Remorseless Winter, Frostmourne Hunger, Howling Blast, Glacial Spikes, Defile, Sindragosa Flyby, and Frozen Throne mechanics.
- Spirit Master now has a multi-spirit encounter structure: Storm, Earth, and Fire pressure phases are tied into a final coordinated fight.
- Banehallow was rebuilt around Soul Harvest, Shadow Line, Soul Rings, Meteor Hunt, Ragna Mark, Darkness, Requiem, Ghost Revenant management, and HP-threshold pressure.
- Magtheridon now uses an ankh/reincarnation encounter loop. Players must manage repeated kills, rupture fragments, respawn empowerment, and difficulty-scaled life counts.

## Rune System

- Runes are a new match system with four visual categories: Recovery, Defense, Offense, and Misc.
- Rune spawns are tied into special wave flow, show map/UI state, and can be claimed once per hero per batch.
- Recovery runes:
  - Healing Rune: team buff for 30 seconds, granting +5% health regen and +8% mana regen.
  - Revitalization Rune: team buff for 20 seconds, reducing active cooldowns by 25% up to 20 seconds and granting +12% mana regen.
  - Restoration Rune: instant team recovery for 35% of missing health and mana.
  - Second Wind Rune: 30-second safety buff that triggers below 30% health, restores 30% health/mana over 3 seconds, and grants a short 20% guard window.
- Defense runes:
  - Barrier Rune: team buff for 30 seconds, granting a shield worth 25% max health and +4% regen.
  - Retaliation Rune: nearby buff for 25 seconds, reflecting 25% of incoming damage.
  - Bulwark Rune: nearby buff for 35 seconds, granting +35 armor and +25% magic resistance.
  - Fortitude Rune: nearby buff for 25 seconds, granting +35% status resistance and reducing incoming damage by 20%.
- Offense runes:
  - Titan Rune: nearby buff for 35 seconds, granting +30% max health, +25% outgoing damage, and a larger model scale.
  - Fury Rune: nearby buff for 30 seconds, granting +160 attack speed and +25% spell amplification.
  - Siegebreaker Rune: nearby buff for 35 seconds, granting +45% damage against creeps and summons.
  - Storm Rune: nearby buff for 30 seconds, striking up to 4 nearby enemies every 1.2 seconds for 550 damage.
- Misc runes:
  - Fragment Rune: instant picker reward tied to Supporter Fragments; currently configured for 50 fragments and flagged for backend validation before publication.
  - Tome Rune: instant team reward granting +50 all stats.
  - Bounty Surge Rune: team buff for 45 seconds, granting +35% shared creep bounty bonus.
  - Momentum Rune: team buff for 45 seconds, granting +18% movement speed and +20% creep gold/XP.
- Rune notifications now announce rune state, claims, expiration, category, and nearby availability.
- Custom rune models and materials were added so rune type is readable in the world, not only in UI text.

## Fragment Quests

- Fragment Quests are optional side objectives that can award stars and fragments during a match.
- General team quests track goals such as War Healers, Coordinated Carnage, Potion Discipline, Potion Panic, Orbs of War, Frontline Pact, No Lane Falls, and Final Stand.
- Event quests track Farm Event Mastery, Muradin Survivors, Ramero and Baristol, Sogat Slayer, Hero Image, All Hero Images, Spirit Beast, and Frost Infernal performance.
- Boss and Phase 3 quests track Clean Assault, Grom Executed, Illidan Hunter, Lich King's End, and Spirit Master completion speed or survival rules.
- Quest progress is pushed to the UI during the match with star states, objective progress, and reward feedback.
- The end screen now shows confirmed Fragment Quest results and fragment gains.
- Death-sensitive quests can count normal deaths and ankh/reincarnation deaths, which makes survival objectives clearer and more enforceable.

## Supporter Pass Foundation

- The Supporter Pass foundation is now present in-game with player tier data, fragment wallet data, season XP, level progress, and public profile surfaces.
- Supporter identity now appears across the experience through tier marks, badges, colors, end-screen rows, top HUD elements, and Wisp-related supporter visuals.
- Supporter vote power is wired into custom setup voting, so supported players can visibly affect difficulty voting according to their tier.
- A Supporter Pass window exists with Rewards, Shop, Armory, Leaderboards, and Settings tabs.
- Rewards, Shop, and Armory are treated as groundwork for 4.1 in the UI, so the 4.0 public note should present this as foundation work rather than a fully launched shop.
- Supporter settings include visible toggles for supporter identity, cosmetic reward display, XP visibility, and companion-related controls.

## UI, HUD, And Quality Of Life

- The top HUD was rebuilt with a fixed ally roster, health/mana state, hero status, supporter marks, tier crests, hover details, ankh charges, reincarnation state, event timers, and difficulty display.
- Overhead status labels now surface important control states such as stunned, hexed, sleep, feared, taunted, cycloned, rooted, leashed, silenced, muted, disarmed, broken, and invulnerable.
- The boss health bar now supports multi-boss fights, cast bars, counters, timers, HP threshold markers, ankh badges, and clearer heal/damage feedback.
- The notification layer was expanded with top and bottom toasts, reward flyouts, rune indicators, Fragment Quest star feedback, special wave countdowns, and main quest completion messaging.
- The end screen now summarizes MVP-style categories such as kills, tome stats, potion usage, boss damage, damage taken, and self-healing.
- End-screen player rows now include richer stats such as net worth, boss damage, damage taken, healing, self-healing, tomes, supporter tier, and pass progress.
- Custom game setup was cleaned around Reborn mode, difficulty voting, ready-up flow, vote progress, and safer player vote handling.
- A buy-max-small-tomes shortcut was added and routed through server-side validation, with restrictions during events or locked arenas.

## Special Events And Match Flow

- Muradin, Farm Event, Ramero and Baristol, and Sogat now connect into the newer timer, notification, reward, rune, and Fragment Quest systems.
- Optional encounters such as Hero Image, All Hero Images, Spirit Beast, and Frost Infernal now have clearer personal timers, return markers, win/fail cleanup, and Fragment Quest hooks.
- Event transitions use more controlled teleport and cinematic pause flows, reducing confusion when players are moved into or out of arenas.
- Tome purchasing and special wave behavior are restricted during specific arenas and events to avoid progression exploits or accidental power spikes.
- Reward flyouts and event timer messaging make event outcomes more visible without requiring players to infer results from chat or hidden state.

## Items And Orbs

- Orb items received a large active/off visual pass, including refreshed item icons and modifier icons.
- New Arcane orb line: spell amp, mana sustain, mana cost reduction, cooldown reduction at higher tiers, and active Arcane Exposure that lowers enemy magic resistance after spells.
- New Venom orb line: attack poison, damage over time, armor reduction at higher tiers, and bonus magic damage when hitting already poisoned enemies.
- New Wind orb line: evasion and movement speed, with upgraded evasion procs that grant movement speed and later damage reduction.
- Earth orb scripting was refreshed around active bash behavior while keeping the line focused on heavy damage and stun pressure.
- Darkness orb scripting was rebuilt around active conversion of slain eligible enemies into temporary controlled units, with higher-tier survivability and damage stats.
- Item image coverage was expanded for Arcane, Venom, Wind, Earth, Darkness, Fire, Frost, Lightning, major utility items, and active/off states.

## Heroes And Abilities

- Archmage now has a Lua Mana Shield implementation that spends mana to absorb incoming damage and turns itself off when mana cannot sustain it.
- Centurion gained Lua implementations for Necromastery and Atrophy Aura, adding soul-based damage scaling and enemy damage suppression themes.
- Huntress/Priestess gained a Lua Arc Lightning implementation with bounce logic, particle feedback, sound, spell absorb handling, and no-repeat target behavior.
- Mountain Giant received a major implementation pass: Craggy Exterior, Earth Splitter, Stone Gaze, growth/model handling, and Toss cleanup were moved toward clearer Lua behavior.
- Wisp received a safer random-hero replacement flow, starting item/gold handling, command restrictions during replacement, and supporter-colored ambient visuals.
- Spirit Master received ability logic for Overload, Ball Lightning, transformation handling, cooldown preservation, tome stat preservation, and remnant/modifier cleanup.
- Lich/Frost abilities received Lua work for Frost Arrows and Frost Beast, including projectile behavior, attack record handling, slow logic, and visual growth handling.
- Additional hero files were touched for smaller ability ports, cleanup, talent/tooltip alignment, or implementation fixes; exact player-facing balance claims should be reviewed before final publication.

## Technical And Content Pipeline

- NPC unit data was split from one very large custom unit file into focused unit files for bosses, events, buildings, summons, wave creeps, special encounters, neutral dragons, allies, VIPs, and dummy units.
- A central precache component was added for bosses, items, runes, UI assets, and other common content groups.
- Custom net table coverage was expanded for runes, Fragment Quests, supporter pass data, overhead status, timers, end-screen stats, and other UI-driven systems.
- Internal devtools were added for faster testing and iteration. This should remain internal-facing in the public note.
- Loading screen and game info assets were refreshed for the 4.0 flow, including new artwork and shared loading-screen sync tooling.
- GitHub/Discord workflow scripts were updated, but these are release operations changes rather than player-facing gameplay content.
